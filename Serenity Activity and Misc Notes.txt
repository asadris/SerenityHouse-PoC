Interesting BI architecture problem worth writing up:
"Temporal perspective filtering in synthetic/demo datasets"

In production, all data is historical or current — future dates don't exist in
operational tables, so measures naturally reflect reality. In a PoC with synthetic
data spanning future dates, you have to explicitly enforce a "as of today" 
perspective throughout the model. This surfaces architectural decisions that are 
invisible in production:
  - Shared date dimensions can't be simply filtered (breaks time intelligence)
  - Filtering at the fact table level cascades correctly through relationships
  - Measures using ALLEXCEPT/REMOVEFILTERS bypass filters silently — need auditing
  - The "correct" solution in production (no future data) hides this complexity

Good example of how synthetic data forces you to understand your model more deeply
than real data would. Relevant for interviews, blog posts, or portfolio writeups
on BI data modeling.
